Street Epistemology

Street Epistemology (SE) is a way to help people critically reflect on the quality of their reasoning through civil conversation. Definition from the Navigating Beliefs course by Street Epistemology International.

Cordial Curiosity

I host Cordial Curiosity, a YouTube channel of cool-headed conversations about what we believe and why. I'm also a co-founder and board member of Street Epistemology International (SEI), the nonprofit behind streetepistemology.com, and one of the main writers of its Navigating Beliefs course.

My story with SE

  1. 2016

    I started making SE content.

  2. 2022

    Peter Boghossian hired me to produce content for his Reverse Q&A tour.

  3. Since

    I created Spectrum Street Epistemology (SSE) and have developed SE with Peter ever since. I am the SSE director for Boghossian.

New Zealand, 2026

An SSE effort in New Zealand. Details coming.
